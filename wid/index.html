WID – When I Die

A private, offline-first digital legacy vault for Android. Store what your loved ones may need — accounts, passwords, documents, wishes — encrypted on your device. Trusted contacts can request access only after a waiting period you set.

One-sentence positioning: WID is the offline vault that holds what matters when you can’t explain it yourself.

Highlights

  • Encrypted local vault (no account required for core use)
  • Master passphrase + optional biometric unlock
  • Trusted contacts & emergency recovery codes
  • Optional encrypted backup (full version)
  • Attachments, categories, security log, auto-lock
