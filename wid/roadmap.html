WID roadmap

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Where WID is headed. Timelines are indicative — privacy and reliability come before new features. The core product stays offline-first; anything involving a network will be optional and explicit.

Current focus: closed / internal beta hardening toward a stable 1.0 with a one-time Full unlock. Feedback from testers shapes the next builds. This page is a public summary, not a contract.

Now — toward 1.0

Hardening the core before the stable release.

Beta hardening Unlock protection, restore without lost drafts, biometric opt-in, security log, and attachment polish.
One-time Full unlock Unlimited contacts and codes, encrypted backup, and higher attachment limits.
Emergency Kit PDF A printable kit with instructions, waiting-period guidance, and an optional code area.
Single-item PDF Share one selected entry without exposing the whole vault.
Store and About polish Clear Play listing, app website links, and complete privacy and data-safety answers.
Stable 1.0 Feature freeze on the core vault, followed by critical fixes only.
Planned

Next — 1.x

Focused improvements after the core is stable.

Richer Emergency Kit QR code support, owner labels, and clearer prepared-for branding.
Biometric choices Choose which supported device biometrics may unlock the vault.
Stronger backups Improved formats, integrity checks, and clearer version handling.
Security log upgrades Richer vault history and optional log export.
Onboarding and accessibility Clearer first-run guidance, larger text, contrast, and screen-reader improvements.
Exploring

Later — 2.0 and beyond

Potential features that require more design work.

Multi-device encrypted sync Optional end-to-end encrypted sync, without changing the offline-first experience. May be subscription-based.
Multiple vaults Separate personal and business vaults, each with its own passphrase and data.
Folder sharing with contacts Share different sets of information with different trusted contacts using finer access controls.
Password-manager import Optional import from supported password managers and Android providers.
iOS version The same privacy model on Apple devices would be a separate, substantial project.
Estate checklist templates Optional non-legal prompts to help organise stored information.
Independent security review Publish more detail on the threat model and results of a qualified third-party review as the product grows.
Version sketch

Release intent

A simplified view of the planned release stages.

0.9.x — Beta Internal and closed-beta hardening.
1.0 — Stable Core vault and one-time Full unlock.
1.1 — Export PDF kit and single-item PDF sharing.
2.0 — Expansion Sync, multiple vaults, or additional platforms if priorities support it.
Not planned

Outside the product boundary

Legal or professional estate advice WID will not replace a will, lawyer, or professional adviser.
Passphrase recovery A forgotten master passphrase cannot be recovered from our side.
Guaranteed emergency access Access cannot be guaranteed if the device is gone and no usable backup exists.

Questions or beta interest? See the beta tester notes or the privacy policy. Development issues are tracked on GitHub.
