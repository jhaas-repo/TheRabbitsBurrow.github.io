WID roadmap

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Where WID is headed. Timelines are indicative — privacy and reliability come before new features. The core product stays offline-first; anything involving a network will be optional and explicit.

Current focus: closed / internal beta hardening toward a stable 1.0 with a one-time Full unlock. Feedback from testers shapes the next builds. This page is a public summary, not a contract.

Now → 1.0

ItemWhat it means
Beta hardening Unlock attempt protection, restore screen after lock (no lost drafts), biometric opt-in, attachments only when you save, fuller security log, type filters and attachment rules polish.
One-time Full unlock Google Play Billing for unlimited contacts & codes, encrypted backup, higher attachment limits.
Emergency Kit as PDF Printable / shareable PDF for trusted contacts (instructions, waiting period, optional code area).
Share a single vault item as PDF Export one entry (e.g. insurance details) when you need to show something without opening the whole vault.
Store & About polish Clear Play listing, website linked from the app, solid privacy & data-safety answers.
Stable 1.0 Feature freeze on the core vault; critical fixes only.

Next (1.x)

ItemWhat it means
Richer Emergency Kit PDF plus optional QR code, owner label, clearer “prepared for” branding.
Biometric options Choose which device biometrics (fingerprint, face, etc.) are allowed for unlock.
Stronger backups Format improvements, integrity checks, clearer version handling.
Security log upgrades Richer history for the vault owner; optional export.
Onboarding & accessibility Clearer first-run guidance, larger text, contrast, and screen-reader pass.

Later

ItemWhat it means
Multi-device encrypted sync Optional sync across your devices with end-to-end encryption (zero-knowledge): the server would only ever see ciphertext. Unlock still needs your master passphrase (or approved device keys). Likely a separate subscription, not part of the one-time Full unlock. Offline-first behaviour remains when you’re offline.
Multiple vaults More than one vault on a device — e.g. personal and business — each with its own passphrase and data.
Folder sharing with contacts Share different sets of information with different trusted contacts (larger access-control design).
Import from password managers Optional import from the phone’s password manager or common managers where the platform allows it.
iOS version Same privacy model on Apple devices — a large, separate effort.
Estate checklist templates Optional non-legal prompts and categories to help organise what you store.
Independent security review If the product grows, publish more detail on the threat model and any third-party review.

Not on the roadmap

  • Replacing a will, lawyer, or professional advice
  • Recovering a forgotten master passphrase from our side
  • Guaranteed access if the device is gone and no backup exists
