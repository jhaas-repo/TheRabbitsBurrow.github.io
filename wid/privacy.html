Privacy Policy for WID – When I Die

Last updated: 11 November 2026
Effective date: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WID – When I Die (“WID”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Android application.

WID is designed as an offline-first personal vault. In normal use, your vault contents stay on your device under your control.

This document is for product transparency. It is not legal advice. Have it reviewed for your jurisdiction (and Google Play requirements) before relying on it as a final policy.

3. Information the app processes

3.1 Information you enter into the vault

This may include, for example:

  • Titles, notes, and categories
  • Usernames, passwords, account numbers, and similar secrets
  • Contact details you store as entries
  • Photos or files you attach
  • Trusted contact names and related settings
  • Emergency access configuration (e.g. waiting period)

Where it is stored: On your device, in an encrypted local database and related local files.
Who can access it: You (via your master passphrase and/or biometric unlock you enable). We do not operate a server that receives your vault contents in the current offline version.

3.2 Security and unlock data on device

To protect the vault, the app may store on device:

  • Cryptographic material derived from your master passphrase (not the passphrase itself in plain text)
  • Encrypted form of keys needed to open the vault
  • Preferences such as auto-lock timeout, theme, and whether onboarding was completed
  • Optional biometric-related unlock material managed with platform security facilities (e.g. Android Keystore / encrypted preferences)

Your master passphrase is not sent to us. If you lose it, we cannot reset it or recover your vault.

3.3 Emergency access

If you generate a recovery code and share an emergency kit with someone you trust:

  • That is a sharing action you initiate
  • The recipient may learn whatever you put in that kit (including a recovery code if you include it)
  • Use of a recovery code can start a waiting period and, after that period, may allow access to the vault on a device where the app and data are present

We do not mediate emergency access through our servers in the current offline product.

3.4 Backups

If you use encrypted backup export/import (full version feature):

  • A backup file is created on your request
  • You choose where to save or send it (device storage, email, cloud drive, etc.)
  • Anyone who obtains that file and the passphrase used to encrypt it may be able to restore its contents

Treat backup files like sensitive documents.

3.5 Device and technical information

Depending on platform behaviour and how you install the app (e.g. Google Play), standard technical data may be processed by the platform or store, such as:

  • Device model, OS version
  • App version, crash reports (if you enable platform crash reporting)
  • Purchase status for paid unlock (processed by Google Play Billing when enabled)

That processing is governed by Google’s policies as well as this policy where it applies to us.

3.6 What we do not intentionally collect (current offline product)

We do not intentionally run a WID backend that collects:

  • Your vault entry contents
  • Your master passphrase
  • Your contacts list from the OS (beyond what you type into WID)
  • Location for vault features
  • Advertising identifiers for ads (we do not show ads)

If this changes (for example optional cloud sync or analytics), we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent or provide opt-out.

4. How we use information

On-device data is used solely to:

  • Provide vault storage, search, and organisation
  • Authenticate you (passphrase / biometric)
  • Support trusted contacts, emergency access, backup, and settings you enable
  • Maintain security logging on the device (e.g. unlock / lock events in the in-app security log)

We do not use vault contents for advertising or profiling.

5. Legal bases (where applicable, e.g. GDPR)

If you are in a region that requires a legal basis:

  • Contract / service delivery: processing needed to provide the app features you request
  • Legitimate interests: on-device security, fraud/abuse prevention for app integrity, improving stability
  • Consent: where we ask for it (e.g. optional features that need it in future)

You may have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, subject to local law. Because vault data is stored on your device, many requests are fulfilled by you using the app (edit/delete entries, clear app data, uninstall).

To exercise rights regarding any data we might hold outside the device (e.g. support emails you send us), contact the email above.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these situations:

  1. You choose to — e.g. share an emergency kit, export a backup, or send a support email that includes details you paste.
  2. Service providers — e.g. Google Play for distribution and payments; device OS security features.
  3. Legal requirements — if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  4. Business transfers — if the app project is transferred, with notice where required.

We do not operate a public directory of users or vaults.

7. Retention

  • On device: Data remains until you delete entries, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or the device is reset.
  • Backups: Retained wherever you stored them, under your control.
  • Support correspondence: Kept only as long as needed to handle your request, unless a longer period is required by law.

8. Security

We implement technical measures appropriate to an offline vault app, including:

  • Encryption of the local database
  • Encryption of sensitive vault payloads
  • Passphrase-based key derivation
  • Optional biometric unlock using platform facilities
  • Auto-lock options

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for:

  • Choosing a strong master passphrase and keeping it secret
  • Controlling who receives recovery codes or backup files
  • Physical security of your device
  • Keeping the app and OS updated

9. Children’s privacy

WID is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data through support channels, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

10. International users

The app stores data primarily on your device. If you export backups or share kits across borders, you are responsible for complying with rules that apply to that transfer.

12. Your choices

  • Do not enter data you are not comfortable storing on the device
  • Disable biometric unlock in Settings if you prefer passphrase only
  • Clear biometric material or change passphrase in Settings
  • Export and delete data before uninstalling if you need a copy
  • Uninstall the app to remove the local vault (backups you created remain where you saved them)
  • Limit who receives emergency codes and kits

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new “Last updated” date. For material changes, we may provide additional notice in the app or store listing where appropriate.
